# CineCircle — Web App Features

CineCircle is a website for people who enjoy movies. Users can discover films, share reviews and lists with friends, and book cinema tickets in one place.

## Features

### Discover movies
Browse movies and search by title or genre. Each movie has its own page with a synopsis, release date, runtime, language, ratings, and reviews. Users can also check where and when the movie is showing.

### Rate and review movies
Users can rate movies, write reviews, and read what others think. They can edit their reviews and like reviews written by other users.

### Talk about reviews
Users can comment on any review, reply to comments, and like other users' comments. A conversation shows two layers at a time; opening a reply shows the replies beneath it. Replies from people you follow are shown first. Users can remove their own comments, and any comment under their own review.

### Keep track of movies
Users can mark movies as watched, record when they watched them, and save movies to a personal watchlist for later.

### Create movie lists
Users can make their own movie lists, such as *Favorite Horror Movies* or *Best Films of the Year*. They can add or remove movies, change their order, and choose whether a list is public or private. From a movie's page, they can add it to a list or start a new list with it. Users can bookmark other users' public lists and find them in their personal corner; public lists with more bookmarks are shown first.

### Follow other users
Users can find friends or other movie fans by name, follow them, visit their profiles, and see their public lists and reviews. Profiles also show watched movies and watchlists, and who follows each user and whom they follow. The community feed can show everyone's reviews or only those of people you follow.

### Notifications
A notification centre gathers comments on your reviews, replies to your comments, likes, new reviews by people you follow, new followers, ticket bookings, a reminder an hour before your screening, and a reminder a week before a premium membership ends. New ones also appear briefly while you browse.

### Find screenings and book tickets
Users can browse cinemas and upcoming screenings, choose a showtime, and view the seats in the screening room. The seat map shows which seats are available. After choosing a seat, users can book a ticket and find it in their ticket history. Tickets cannot be refunded or exchanged once booked, and the booking page says so before you confirm.

### Premium membership and discounts
The website has regular and premium accounts; every user is exactly one of the two. Premium members receive a discount on eligible tickets for 12 months, and users can see any applicable offers before booking. Users can choose premium when they register, upgrade from their personal corner, or upgrade at checkout, where a regular user sees what the same seats would cost a premium member. When a membership ends, the user becomes a regular user again and can renew.

### Account settings
Users can change their username, email and password in their account settings. A username or email that another user already has is refused with an alert. Changing the password needs the current password and the new password entered twice. Users can also delete their account; because this cannot be undone, it asks for confirmation twice, the second time with their password. Their tickets are kept as sales records without their name or email.

## Example User Journey

A user finds a movie on CineCircle, reads a few reviews, and adds it to their watchlist. Later, they check nearby cinema listings, pick a screening, choose an available seat, and book a ticket. After watching the movie, they mark it as watched, give it a rating, and write a review. Other users can like the review, follow them, or explore their public movie lists.
