# CineCircle — Web App Features

CineCircle is a website for people who enjoy movies. Users can discover films, share reviews and lists with friends, and book cinema tickets in one place.

## Features

### Discover movies
Browse movies and search by title or genre. Each movie has its own page with a synopsis, release date, runtime, language, ratings, and reviews. Users can also check where and when the movie is showing.

### Rate and review movies
Users can rate movies, write reviews, and read what others think. They can edit their reviews and like reviews written by other users.

### Keep track of movies
Users can mark movies as watched, record when they watched them, and save movies to a personal watchlist for later.

### Create movie lists
Users can make their own movie lists, such as *Favorite Horror Movies* or *Best Films of the Year*. They can add or remove movies, change their order, and choose whether a list is public or private.

### Follow other users
Users can follow friends or other movie fans, visit their profiles, and see their public lists and reviews. Profiles also show watched movies and watchlists.

### Find screenings and book tickets
Users can browse cinemas and upcoming screenings, choose a showtime, and view the seats in the screening room. The seat map shows which seats are available. After choosing a seat, users can book a ticket and find it in their ticket history.

### Premium membership and discounts
The website has regular and premium accounts. Premium members receive a discount on eligible tickets, and users can see any applicable offers before booking.

## Example User Journey

A user finds a movie on CineCircle, reads a few reviews, and adds it to their watchlist. Later, they check nearby cinema listings, pick a screening, choose an available seat, and book a ticket. After watching the movie, they mark it as watched, give it a rating, and write a review. Other users can like the review, follow them, or explore their public movie lists.
