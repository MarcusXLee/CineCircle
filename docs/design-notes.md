# Visual direction and asset credits

CineCircle presents its demo catalogue as a film programme in one house style: a charcoal ground, champagne gilt as the only accent, pearl text, and cream tickets printed in claret ink. Controls have fixed roles: filled champagne satin buttons do something (save, publish, follow, add); gilt hairline buttons go somewhere or offer a quiet option; filters and the current page are hairline pills that turn gilt when chosen; anything on a ticket is flat claret ink with an inner printed rule. Tickets follow how real ones are made: the stock is pre-printed in claret (house name, ADMIT ONE, field labels), the screening's details are stamped on in Courier Prime typewriter ink with a slight bleed, serial numbers are struck in red, tear lines are punched holes, and printed buttons carry a fine ink speckle. This applies to the home ticket, the booking ticket and the stubs in My tickets. The hairline pills (concierge and secondary buttons, the header search, the current page and the genre chips) are frosted glass: a soft upper sheen, a bright top edge, a darker lower edge and a low shadow, turning gilt-edged on hover or when chosen. Rating stars are dark golden foil without an outline, the same material as the hot-stamped quote (a shared SVG gradient and grain filter in App.vue; a bevelled brass version is defined there too). Page and dialog titles and empty-state messages use Baskervville; typing prompts are plain, the header search's in small spaced capitals; section headings keep Bricolage Grotesque; seats are claret velvet, premium seats (the middle of rows D to F) are black velvet with gold piping, and the chosen seat is gilt. Comments open as a quiet thread under a review, behind a gilt hairline, two layers at a time: opening a reply sets that comment above its own replies, a step larger, with a gilt Back link. Like, Reply and a bin sit together under each comment; replies from people you follow lead, tagged with a small gilt Following pill. A collection's page has a Bookmark button that turns gilt once saved, and cards show the bookmark count beside a small gilt ribbon. A bell in the header carries a gilt count and opens a glass panel of notifications, each with its medallion icon; new ones arrive as a toast. The home page opens with a spotlight of six films (Casablanca, Rear Window, In the Mood for Love, Singin' in the Rain, Roman Holiday and La La Land) that moves on every seven seconds and loops back to the first. It waits while the pointer is over it or someone moves through it with the keyboard, and stays still under reduced motion. The frame is close to 16:9, so each still fills it almost as the film framed it and dissolves into the dark on the side that holds the text. The layout follows the still: Casablanca and Singin' in the Rain set their text on the right, the others on the left, so the fade covers only the calm part of each picture and no face. A film can also tune its own title size and height (Casablanca's single-word title is set larger and a little higher, Roman Holiday's very slightly), and La La Land adds a line from the film, “Here’s to the fools who dream.”, credited to Mia Dolan, as an italic pull quote, hot-stamped in near-white silver foil with a fine grain, as bright as plain white text; the credit is plain gilt small capitals. Gilt arrows appear only when the pointer reaches either side; touch screens swipe. Beside it, the selected film's next screening is printed as a cream admission ticket with punched corners, a tear line and a stub. A public community list closes the page, its covers fading into the background. The controls a visitor meets first (Film details, Sign in and the header search) use the concierge style: glass pills with pearl small capitals. Choose seats is printed on the ticket instead: flat claret ink with a thin rule inside its edge. Decorative icons sit in gilded, lightly grained medallions; tabs are a recessed satin track with a raised pearl tab and a small gilt diamond marking the current choice. Film stills also appear behind the film page header. Above the seat map, the screen is drawn as a gilded curve. The dark seating chart uses a check for selected seats and a hatched cross for unavailable seats. The booking summary uses a light paper background.

Cinzel sets the CineCircle wordmark, Baskervville sets the featured film and page titles, Bricolage Grotesque sets section headings, and DM Sans sets reviews and controls. All four fonts are bundled locally through Fontsource. The design draws on [Anthropic’s Frontend Design skill](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design) and [Vercel’s Web Design Guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills/tree/main/skills/web-design-guidelines).

All media is stored locally in `public/images`, so normal application use makes no third-party media/font requests. The artwork belongs to the respective film rights holders and is included for this educational local demonstration. Obtain appropriate permissions or replacements before public redistribution.
