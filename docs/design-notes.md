# Visual direction and asset credits

CineCircle presents its demo catalogue as a film programme on a charcoal ground with lavender controls. The home page opens with a spotlight of six films, switched from a small film reel; each still fades into the page behind a Baskervville title. Beside it, the selected film's next screening is printed as a cream admission ticket with punched corners, a tear line and a stub. A public community list closes the page, its covers fading into the background. Decorative icons sit in gilded, lightly grained medallions; tabs are a recessed satin track with a raised pearl tab and a small gilt diamond marking the current choice. Film stills also appear behind the film page header. Above the seat map, the screen is drawn as a gilded curve. The dark seating chart uses a check for selected seats and a hatched cross for unavailable seats. The booking summary uses a light paper background.

Cinzel sets the CineCircle wordmark, Baskervville sets the featured film and page titles, Bricolage Grotesque sets section headings, and DM Sans sets reviews and controls. All four fonts are bundled locally through Fontsource. The design draws on [Anthropic’s Frontend Design skill](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design) and [Vercel’s Web Design Guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills/tree/main/skills/web-design-guidelines).

All media is stored locally in `public/images`, so normal application use makes no third-party media/font requests. The artwork belongs to the respective film rights holders and is included for this educational local demonstration. Obtain appropriate permissions or replacements before public redistribution.
